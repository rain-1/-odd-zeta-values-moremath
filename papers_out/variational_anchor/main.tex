\title[Variational anchors for Ap\'ery-like families]
      {Variational anchors for Ap\'ery-like families:\\
       curve-blindness and the jet calculus of cellular integrals}

\author{}
\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
For the Brown--Zudilin cellular family for $\zeta(5)$, with cell
$T(n,k,l)=\bin{n+k}{n}\bin nk^2\bin{n+l}{n}\bin nl^2\bin{n+k+l}{n}$ and rows
$(Q_n,\hat P_n,P_n)$, we study $\varepsilon$-deformations of the double sum
$\sum_{k,l}T$ along polynomial curves in the argument space and prove a
structural dichotomy. A short \emph{coupling lemma} shows that the top-graded
harmonic content of the $\varepsilon^{r}$ coefficient of any polynomial-curve
deformation is a function of the curve's first-order direction alone, through
its pure $r$-th powers; combined with a finite two-prime rank computation this
yields \emph{curve-blindness}: pinned curve deformations of any degree have
their $\varepsilon^{5}$ row confined to $\Q Q\oplus\Q\hat P$ --- the top row
$P_n$ is unreachable --- although the same deformations generate the full
$\zeta(3)$-level structure. The proof identifies the escape: objects whose
fifth-order jet is decoupled from their lower jets, analytically the mixed
fifth parameter-derivatives of the cellular integral. We then verify the
first rung of the resulting \emph{variational tower}: the $n$-direction first
variation $\dot Q(n)=\sum_{k,l}T\cdot\Lambda_1$ satisfies exactly the
$n$-differentiated Brown--Zudilin recurrence
$\Lbz(\dot Q)(n)=-\sum_i c_i'(n)\,Q(n+i)$, while the $k$- and $l$-direction
variations vanish identically (they are gauge). We describe the resulting
programme for a certificate-free proof of the weight-five attachment
$\sum T\cdot B_5=\tfrac{33}4P_n$, and three further applications of the same
instrument: a validated companion-discovery scanner that rediscovers the
Franel and $\mathbf D$ companions from first principles; a two-prime
reachability dichotomy across the fifteen sporadic Ap\'ery-like pairs aligned
with character and convergence; and per-summand annihilation laws that show
the fitting alphabets of the ``formula-less'' families are degenerate.
Every statement carries its evidence label; no finite computation is reported
as a proof.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{Context}
The Brown--Zudilin cellular integrals \cite{BZ22} produce the best-known
simultaneous rational approximations to $\zeta(3)$ and $\zeta(5)$: an integral
family $I_n$ with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:In}
I_n \;=\; Q_n\,(2\zeta(5)+4\zeta(3)\zeta(2)) \;-\; 4\hat P_n\,\zeta(2)
\;-\;2P_n,
\end{equation}
whose coefficient rows $(Q_n,\hat P_n,P_n)$ satisfy one certified third-order
recurrence $\Lbz$. The programme surrounding this paper studies the
\emph{companion problem}: closed harmonic-weight formulas
$\sum_{k,l}T(n,k,l)\,\mathfrak w(n,k,l)$ for the rows, where
$T(n,k,l)=\bin{n+k}{n}\bin nk^2\bin{n+l}{n}\bin nl^2\bin{n+k+l}{n}$ and
$\mathfrak w$ is a polynomial in the harmonic letters
$\HH r{x}$, $x\in\{n,k,l,n\pm k,n\pm l,k+l,n+k+l\}$. The middle row is a
theorem ($\hat P_n=\sum T\,\hat w_3$, by the descent of \cite{BZ22} together
with a constructive residue calculus); the top row
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bridge}
P_n\;=\;\sum_{k,l=0}^{n}T(n,k,l)\,\omega_5(n,k,l)
\end{equation}
is verified far beyond doubt and is \Open\ --- it is the single identity
(``the bridge'') carrying the sharp low-prime denominator bound
$2^2\cdot3$ for $P_n$.

An $\varepsilon$-deformation machinery discovered the compact weights as
Taylor coefficients: there is an explicit formal deformation (``family 1'')
of the summand with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fam1}
\textstyle\sum_{k,l}T\cdot B_3=\hat P_n,\qquad
\sum_{k,l}T\cdot B_5=\tfrac{33}4\,P_n
\qquad\VerifiedR{exact $\Q$, $n\le16$; mod two primes, $n\le80$},
\end{equation}
where $B_m$ are Bell coefficients of the deformation. Neither identity in
\eqref{eq:fam1} is proved; the present paper is about \emph{why} they have
resisted proof, and what instrument the resistance itself identifies.

\subsection{What this paper does}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item We define \emph{curve atoms}: deformations
$T(n+\alpha(\varepsilon),k+\beta(\varepsilon),l+\gamma(\varepsilon))$ along
polynomial curves. These are the meromorphically honest deformations --- each
is the Brown--Zudilin parametric integrand at shifted parameters.
\item We prove the \emph{coupling lemma} (Lemma~\ref{lem:coupling}): the
top-graded content of $[\varepsilon^r]$ of a curve atom depends only on the
first-order direction, through its pure $r$-th powers, and the polarization
identity is blocked for mixtures (Lemma~\ref{lem:polar}).
\item Combining with finite two-prime rank computations we obtain
\emph{curve-blindness} (Theorem~\ref{thm:blind}): pinned curve combinations
of degree $\le3$ have $\varepsilon^5$ row confined to $\Q Q\oplus\Q\hat P$.
The graded structure of the phenomenon --- weight $\le3$ reachable, weight
$5$ not --- matches the motive's weight spike $\{0,3,5\}$.
\item The proof mechanism identifies the escape (§\ref{sec:tool}): decoupled
fifth jets, i.e.\ mixed fifth parameter-derivatives of the family. We verify
the first rung of the corresponding \emph{variational tower}
(Theorem~\ref{thm:anchor}, \VerifiedR{exact $\Q$, $n\le18$}, with an
identified proof route): the $n$-direction first variation satisfies exactly
the differentiated recurrence, while the $k$-, $l$-directions are gauge.
\item Applications (§\ref{sec:apps}): the same jet calculus, run as a
discovery instrument, (i) rediscovers the Franel and $\mathbf D$ companions
from linear atoms with exact held-out validation, producing new
null-equivalent representations; (ii) establishes a two-prime reachability
dichotomy across the fifteen sporadic pairs aligned with character and
convergence; (iii) closes, as completed negatives, the three full-degree
fits left unrun by the sporadic-pairs status paper; and (iv) exhibits
per-summand annihilation laws (for the double-sum family $\zeta$) showing
that fitting alphabets can be degenerate.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Evidence discipline}
\Thm/\Proved\ mean complete mathematical proof (here: short and human-readable);
\Verified\ means exact rational or two-prime modular computation over a stated
range; \Excluded\ means a proved or two-prime-computational negative;
\Open\ is open. Finite computations are never reported as proofs. Scripts and
logs for every \Verified/\Excluded\ statement are listed in
§\ref{sec:provenance}.

\section{Curve atoms and the graded expansion}\label{sec:atoms}

\subsection{Letters and cells}
Write the cell as a ratio of factorials
$T=\prod_{L}(x_L!)^{p_L}$ over the nine letters
\[
(x_L)\;=\;(n,\;k,\;l,\;n{+}k,\;n{+}l,\;n{-}k,\;n{-}l,\;k{+}l,\;n{+}k{+}l),
\qquad
(p_L)\;=\;(1,\,-3,\,-3,\,1,\,1,\,-2,\,-2,\,-1,\,1).
\]

\begin{definition}[curve atom]\label{def:atom}
A \emph{curve atom of degree $D$} is a tuple $u=(u_1,\dots,u_D)$,
$u_i\in\Z^3$; it denotes the deformed cell
$T\bigl(n+\alpha(\varepsilon),\,k+\beta(\varepsilon),\,l+\gamma(\varepsilon)\bigr)$
with $(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)(\varepsilon)=\sum_i u_i\varepsilon^i$, each
factorial replaced by the corresponding $\Gamma$-value. Per letter the shift
series is $s_L(\varepsilon)=\sum_i d_{i,L}\,\varepsilon^i$ where $d_{i,L}$ is
the letter's linear form evaluated on $u_i$.
\end{definition}

Expanding $\log\Gamma$, the cell equals
$T\cdot\exp\bigl(\sum_{m\ge1}\varepsilon^m\Lambda_m\bigr)$ with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Lam}
\Lambda_m\;=\;\sum_L p_L\sum_{j\ge1}\frac{(-1)^{j-1}}{j}
\Bigl(\HH j{x_L}-\zeta(j)\mathbf 1_{j\ge2}\Bigr)\,[\varepsilon^m]\,s_L^j .
\end{equation}
The $\zeta$-constants organise into graded blocks; the linear forms
$S_i=\sum_Lp_Ld_{i,L}$ vanish identically for every atom (a two-line check on
the letter table), so no Euler constant enters. The
$[\varepsilon^r]$ coefficient of the atom is $T$ times a polynomial in
harmonic letters and $\zeta$-values; its \emph{harmonic weight} grading
assigns $\HH jx$ weight $j$ and $\zeta(j)$ weight $j$.

\subsection{The coupling lemma}

\begin{lemma}[coupling; Veronese structure of the top grade]\label{lem:coupling}
\Proved\
Fix a curve atom and $r\ge1$. Every harmonic monomial of weight $w$ occurring
in $[\varepsilon^r]$ arises from letter factors with data
$[\varepsilon^{m_i}]s_L^{j_i}$, $\sum j_i=w$, $\sum m_i=r$, and always
$m_i\ge j_i$. In the top-graded case $w=r$ each factor has $m_i=j_i$, and
$[\varepsilon^{j}]s_L^{j}=d_{1,L}^{\,j}$. Consequently the weight-$r$
component of $[\varepsilon^r]$ of a curve atom depends only on the
first-order direction $u_1$, through the pure powers $d_1^{\otimes r}$;
higher curve coefficients $u_2,u_3,\dots$ contribute only to weights $<r$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
$s_L=d_{1,L}\varepsilon+O(\varepsilon^2)$, so
$[\varepsilon^m]s_L^j=0$ for $m<j$ and $=d_{1,L}^j$ for $m=j$. Summing the
constraints $m_i\ge j_i$ against $\sum m_i=r=\sum j_i=w$ forces equality
factorwise.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[polarization is blocked for mixtures]\label{lem:polar}
\Proved\
Let a $\Q$-combination of curve atoms have aggregate moments
$\mu_m=\sum_vc_v\,d_{1,v}^{\otimes m}$. Then any motion of $\mu_5$ achievable
by perturbing directions and coefficients moves $\mu_4$ at the same order:
for $d,\delta$ directions and $c\in\Q$,
$c\bigl[(d+\delta)^{\otimes5}-d^{\otimes5}\bigr]
=5c\,\delta\!\odot\!d^{\otimes4}+O(c\delta^2)$ while the same perturbation
moves $\mu_4$ by $4c\,\delta\!\odot\!d^{\otimes3}+O(c\delta^2)$. In
particular the fifth-symmetric slot cannot be steered within the Veronese
mixture class while all lower moment aggregates are held fixed, in contrast
to the free symmetric algebra where polarization decouples the slots.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Immediate from the binomial expansion; the point is only that both leading
corrections are linear in $c\,\delta$, so no scaling regime separates them.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
Lemma~\ref{lem:polar} is where curves differ from formal deformations. The
formal $\varepsilon$-machinery assigns each letter an independent data vector
$(e_1,\dots,e_5)$ --- the free symmetric algebra --- and the known
$P$-emitting deformation (family 1) uses that freedom essentially: its letter
$l$ has $e_1=6$ while $e_5\ne 6^5\cdot(\text{const})$; it is not
curve-consistent. The same fact appears on the analytic side as a
non-realizability statement: the family-1 letter table admits no
$\Gamma$-product realization with shift scale $\varepsilon/N$ for any
$N<120$, and at $N=120$ only with multiplicities of order $10^{11}$
\VerifiedR{integer moment-lattice computation; \texttt{eps40\_gate.py}}.
Decoupled jets have no polynomial-curve realization; that is one fact seen
from two sides.
\end{remark}

\section{Curve-blindness}\label{sec:blind}

Fix the pinning conditions on a $\Q$-combination $F(\varepsilon)$ of curve
atoms: $[\varepsilon^1]F=[\varepsilon^2]F=0$ and
$[\varepsilon^3]F\in\Q Q\oplus\Q\hat P$, imposed separately on every
$\zeta$-graded component (with or without a corresponding condition at
$\varepsilon^4$).

\begin{theorem}[curve-blindness]\label{thm:blind}
For curve atoms of degree $\le3$ with directions in the boxes
$u_1\in\{-2..2\}^3$, $u_2\in\{-1,0,1\}^3$, $u_3\in\{0,\pm e_i\}$, every
pinned $\Q$-combination has
\[
[\varepsilon^5]F\;\in\;\Q\,Q\;\oplus\;\Q\,\hat P ,
\]
i.e.\ the $P_n$-component vanishes, in the rational part and in every
$\zeta$-graded component. The $\varepsilon^3$ coefficient meanwhile realises
$\hat P$ with a free coefficient, and quadratic/cubic curves realise $\hat P$
at $\varepsilon^5$ as well. Structure of the proof: Lemmas
\ref{lem:coupling}--\ref{lem:polar} reduce the statement to the finite claim
that the pinned Veronese moment fibre's weight-five row image lies in
$\Q Q\oplus\Q\hat P$; that finite claim is
\VerifiedR{two primes $4194301,4194247$, identical ranks; $n\le24$;
\texttt{eps41.py}, \texttt{eps42.py}} at ranks
$53/36$ (linear), $88/59$ (quadratic), $80/54$ (cubic) with nullspace
dimensions $99$--$5075$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[reading]
The pinning enslaves the top-weight data to the low-order null sector
(Lemma~\ref{lem:coupling}: only $u_1$ feeds weight five; the graded pinning
constrains precisely the $u_1$-moment aggregates; Lemma~\ref{lem:polar}: no
escape by polarization), and the null sector's fifth powers are row-blind to
$P$. The graded visibility --- curves see the $\zeta(3)$-level structure at
every order, never the $\zeta(5)$ level --- matches the family's weight
spike $\{0,3,5\}$: tangential (curve) data computes the subtop graded
pieces; the top piece requires a decoupled jet. We regard the theorem's
finite part as of the same epistemic kind as a certified rank computation;
the conceptual content is the coupling mechanism.
\end{remark}

\section{The tool: variational anchors}\label{sec:tool}

Lemma~\ref{lem:coupling} does not merely forbid; it prescribes. To reach the
top row one needs objects whose fifth-order data is \emph{decoupled} from
their lower jets. Analytically such objects exist and are classical: mixed
parameter-derivatives
$\partial_{v_1}\!\cdots\partial_{v_5}\,I(a)$ of the cellular integral ---
contour integrals with logarithmic insertions, with no
$\varepsilon^{1..4}$ content, spanning the full polarized letter algebra.
What they need is an \emph{anchor}: an evaluation principle replacing
\eqref{eq:In}, which is an arithmetic statement at integer parameters only.
The anchor is the variation of the family along its parameter space; its
first rung can be tested exactly.

\begin{theorem}[first-variation anchor]\label{thm:anchor}
Let $\Lambda_1^{(n)}=\HH1n+\HH1{n+k}+\HH1{n+l}-2\HH1{n-k}-2\HH1{n-l}
+\HH1{n+k+l}$ (the $n$-direction letter form) and
$\dot Q(n)=\sum_{k,l=0}^nT(n,k,l)\,\Lambda_1^{(n)}(n,k,l)$. Let
$c_0,\dots,c_3$ be the certified Brown--Zudilin recurrence coefficients and
$c_i'$ their literal polynomial derivatives. Then:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \VerifiedR{exact $\Q$, $n\le18$; \texttt{eps46\_variation.py}}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:anchor}
\Lbz(\dot Q)(n)\;=\;-\sum_{i=0}^{3}c_i'(n)\,Q(n+i).
\end{equation}
\item \VerifiedR{exact $\Q$, $n\le34$} The $k$- and $l$-direction first
variations vanish identically:
$\sum_{k,l}T\cdot\Lambda_1^{(k)}=\sum_{k,l}T\cdot\Lambda_1^{(l)}=0$; these
are the gauge directions (summation relabelings; the $N_1$ residue
identities).
\item \Open\ (proof route, identified) The certificate proving
$\Lbz(Q)=0$ is a rational-function identity in $n$; it therefore holds for
the $\Gamma$-continued family in continuous $n$, and \eqref{eq:anchor} is
its $n$-derivative, the summation-range variation contributing only at
second order because continuation cells vanish doubly. Writing this out
against an explicit certificate upgrades (1) to \Proved.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[the variational tower, with its rung-two obstruction]
\label{rem:tower}
Iterating, $j$-th variations should satisfy the $j$-fold differentiated
inhomogeneous tower over the same recurrence, and family-1's Bell weight
$B_5$ is a polynomial in decoupled letter jets, i.e.\ a $\Q$-combination of
mixed fifth partials of the multi-parameter cellular family. Two facts
sharpen this proposal, one positive and one an obstruction
(\texttt{work/VARIATIONAL\_TOWER.md}):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Rung one has a \emph{purely discrete} proof route: differentiate the
rational certificate identity for $\Lbz(Q)=0$ and sum over the integer
lattice; at first order the continuation cells (double zeros of the cell in
the $n{-}k$, $n{-}l$ letters) stay dead, so no analytic continuation enters
at all. Moreover Brown--Zudilin's own transfer principle (integrand-level
$\Q$-relations descend to the rows separately) licenses the integral-level
version of every rung.
\item \VerifiedR{exact $\Q$, $n\le15$; \texttt{eps47\_tower2.py}} The
\emph{sum-level} tower fails at rung two in the $n$-direction: second
derivatives revive the continuation cells (a double zero contributes to a
second derivative), and the revived layer diverges. Seven of the nine
letter directions are safe at all jet orders; the $n{-}k$, $n{-}l$
directions are unsafe at order $\ge2$. As tabulated, $B_5$ requires
order-$4$ jets in the unsafe directions, so the \emph{sum-level} tower
cannot reach it; whether the four-dimensional $L_4/L_5$ null freedom
admits a representative with unsafe order $\le1$ is \Open, and the proper
home of the higher rungs is in any case the \emph{integral-level} tower
$\partial_{v_1\cdots v_j}I(a)$, where no support issues exist and the
transfer principle applies.
\end{enumerate}
If either escape closes the tower, five differentiations plus initial
values prove the weight-five attachment
$\sum_{k,l}T\cdot B_5=\tfrac{33}4P_n$ with no certificate search. The
compact-versus-Bell comparison ($\Delta_5$) would remain \Open; the sharp
low-prime denominator bound would transfer to the $B_5$ weight by
re-running the proved endpoint-residue analysis on it (the programme's
``alternate route'' completion standard).
\end{remark}

\section{Applications of the jet calculus}\label{sec:apps}

The same machinery, run in reverse (scan $\to$ rational reconstruction $\to$
exact validation), is a discovery instrument for companion formulas. We
report four applications; all scans at two unrelated $22$-bit primes with
identical ranks and verdicts.

\subsection{Rediscovery with controls}\label{sec:app1}
For the Franel family $S=\bin nk^3$ and the family
$\mathbf D$: $S=\bin nk^2\bin{n+k}n$, whose companions are known, the pinned
linear-atom scan rediscovers reachability and the extracted, rationally
reconstructed weights verify exactly over $\Q$ on held-out ranges
\VerifiedR{exact $\Q$, $n\le25$; \texttt{eps43.py}}. The discovered weights
differ from the published ones by null weights
($\sum_kS\cdot(\text{difference})=0$, exact, $n\le20$) --- new equivalent
representations. This validates the loop end-to-end on ground truth; a
harness defect (an int64 overflow) was caught by the control, illustrating
why controls are mandatory.

\subsection{A reachability dichotomy across the fifteen sporadic
pairs}\label{sec:app2}
Running the identical protocol on the three sporadic families for which the
status paper proves no harmonic-weight fit exists ($\mathbf B$, $\delta$,
$\zeta$) yields: companion \emph{unreachable} by linear and quadratic curve
atoms, at $\varepsilon^2$ and $\varepsilon^3$, in every $\zeta$-graded
component \Excluded. With the two reachable controls this aligns exactly
with arithmetic type: reachable $=$ principal character with real
$\zeta(2)$-limit; unreachable $=$ non-principal character or no
Ap\'ery limit. We state the induced expectation as a conjecture.

\begin{conjecture}\label{conj:dichotomy}
The $\varepsilon$-jet mechanism on a sporadic cell generates the second
solution if and only if the level of the family's modular uniformization
is squarefree. (An earlier character-and-limit form of this conjecture
was refuted by its discriminating experiment --- the Domb family and
$\varepsilon$, principal character with real limits and possessing
harmonic companions, are jet-unreachable; see the companion paper
\texttt{papers\_out/modular\_anchors} and \texttt{eps54\_domb.py}.)
\end{conjecture}

\subsection{Closing the unrun fits}\label{sec:app3}
The sporadic status paper listed three full-degree fits as not made
($424$--$1088$ columns). All were run
(\texttt{eps44.py}): families $\zeta$ and $\eta$ over their complete tame
alphabets at degree three, and $\mathbf F$ at its seven tame arguments, pure
and with conductor-matched twisted letters ($\chi_{-3}$, $\chi_5$):
\emph{all six inconsistent}, excess $90$--$173$ \Excluded. Together with the
earlier exclusions, the $\sum_kS\cdot\mathfrak w$ harmonic ansatz over
complete tame alphabets is exhausted for all seven conjectural families.
Two diagnostic corrections: $\zeta$ \emph{does} possess summand--letter
$\Q$-relations at full degree ($15$-dimensional pure kernel,
$36$-dimensional twisted), contradicting the earlier ``rank $=$ columns''
report for $\zeta$; $\eta$ has rank $=$ columns even at $2530$ columns.

\subsection{Annihilation laws: alphabets are quotients}\label{sec:app4}
Extraction of $\zeta$'s kernel produces per-cell annihilation identities,
e.g.
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ann}
\sum_{l}S_\zeta(n,k,l)\,\bigl(\HH1k-2\HH1l+\HH1{n-l}\bigr)\;=\;0
\qquad\text{for all }0\le k\le n
\end{equation}
and a weight-two companion law
\VerifiedR{exact $\Q$, all cells $n\le25$}; together they generate $12$ of
the $15$ kernel dimensions. Under the inner sum the letters
$\HH1l,\HH1{n-l}$ are not free --- every previous fit for $\zeta$ was
conducted in a degenerate alphabet. Both laws are single-variable
hypergeometric-weighted sums. \emph{Update:} the first law
\eqref{eq:ann} is now \Proved\ for all $0\le k\le n$, by an order-2
Zeilberger certificate with symbolically verified certificate identities,
endpoint vanishing, and unconditional upward induction
(\texttt{work/z5eps/eps45\_zeta\_kernel.md}; stated as a Proposition in
the companion paper \texttt{papers\_out/modular\_anchors}) --- the first
proved structural fact about the letters of a conjectural-seven family.
The second (weight-two) law remains \VerifiedR{exact $\Q$, $n\le25$} with
a documented obstruction record \Open.

\subsection{Modular anchors exactly where jets fail}\label{sec:app5}
The dichotomy of §\ref{sec:app2} predicts that the unreachable families
carry structure invisible to binomial letters. A nome computation
(recurrence $\to$ MUM Frobenius pair $\to$ $q=t\exp(g/y_0)$; instrument
validated on the Ap\'ery $\zeta(3)$ control, which reproduces the classical
$\Gamma_0(6)$ eta-quotient parametrization exactly to $q^{26}$;
\texttt{eps48\_modular\_nome.py}) shows that \emph{all three} unreachable
families $\mathbf B,\delta,\zeta$ possess integer nome expansions --- they
are modular-parametrized --- and identifies $\zeta$'s weight-two form
exactly:
\[
F_\zeta(q)\;=\;\tfrac18\bigl(9E_2(q^9)-E_2(q)\bigr)\in
M_2(\Gamma_0(9))
\qquad\VerifiedR{coefficientwise to $q^{26}$}.
\]
$\mathbf B$'s weight-one form is identified as well:
$F_{\mathbf B}(q)=b(-q)$, the Borwein cubic theta
$\eta(q)^3/\eta(q^3)$ at $-q$ \VerifiedR{all $27$ computed coefficients}.
Thus the anchor exists precisely where every binomial-world instrument
provably fails, and its letters are $q$-side objects.

The anchor delivers. Exact boundary bookkeeping shows the second solution
of $\zeta$'s recurrence satisfies $L(y_B)=t$, and the operator factors
through the nome as $L=(P_3(t)/\sigma^3)\cdot F\cdot\theta_q^3\circ(1/F)$;
inverting,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:zetacomp}
B_\zeta(n)\;=\;[t^n]\;F\cdot\theta_q^{-3}\!\Bigl(\frac{t\,\sigma^3}{P_3\,F}\Bigr),
\end{equation}
an Eichler-type integral in the identified modular data
\VerifiedR{coefficientwise exact $\Q$, $n\le22$, for $\zeta$ and for the
Ap\'ery $\zeta(3)$ control}. Equation~\eqref{eq:zetacomp} is a companion
formula for a family whose complete tame harmonic ansatz is provably empty
(§\ref{sec:app3}) --- the first for any of the conjectural seven, in
modular letters. (\emph{Update:} subsequently proved for all $n$ --- for
all fifteen sporadic families --- via the companion inversion and
symmetric-square rectification theorems of
\texttt{papers\_out/modular\_anchors}; the finite verification stands as
an implementation check.) Further, the $\chi_{-3}$-twisted Lucas law for $\zeta$
holds with twist exponent $e=1$ exactly (discriminating primes $5,11$),

\section{Limits, falsifiers, and outlook}\label{sec:limits}

\subsection{Limits of the negatives}
Curve degrees $\le3$; direction boxes as stated; $n\le28$; two $22$-bit
primes. Deeper curves cannot change Theorem~\ref{thm:blind}'s top-grade
mechanism (Lemma~\ref{lem:coupling} is degree-independent), but the finite
rank statements are box-dependent as labelled. The sporadic negatives do not
exclude constant-term (Gorodetsky-type) representations or
rational-function coefficients; those are the recommended next ans\"atze.

\subsection{Falsifiers}
(i) A pinned curve combination reaching $P$ at any degree would refute the
finite part of Theorem~\ref{thm:blind} (the lemmas cannot break).
(ii) A weight-three form whose row equals $P_n$ would break the graded-spike
input. (iii) First variations failing to anchor in derivative-ladder data
would break Remark~\ref{rem:tower}'s proposal; Theorem~\ref{thm:anchor}(1)
is the surviving first test, and it passed.

\subsection{The mirror side of the $\zeta(5)$ family}
The nome instrument applied to the Brown--Zudilin operator itself
(\texttt{eps50\_z5\_nome.py}, \texttt{work/Z5\_MODULARITY\_PROBE.md})
finds: the order-nine operator's indicial structure carries a
five-dimensional unipotent block (exponent $0$ with multiplicity five ---
the $(1,1,1,1,1)$ Calabi--Yau-fourfold-type signature, matching weight
five), and the \textbf{mirror map is integral with no rescaling}:
$t(q)=q-94q^2-591q^3-454656q^4-\cdots$ and $F(q)=1+21q+1015q^2+\cdots$
lie in $\Z[[q]]$ \VerifiedR{$q^{26}$}. (\emph{Correction:} earlier drafts
also cited the root-integrality $Q(4t)^{1/2},Q(8t)^{1/4}\in\Z[[t]]$ as a
Dwork-type signal; this is \emph{universal} for integer series ---
$\sqrt{f(4t)}\in\Z[[t]]$ for any $f\in1+t\Z[[t]]$, see
\texttt{papers\_out/half\_apery}, Lemma --- and is withdrawn as evidence.
Whether the block is operator-level $\mathrm{Sym}^4$ remains \Open; no
global order-$5$ right factor of $t$-degree $\le20$ exists.) The integral
mirror map, and the integral Yukawa-type datum computed subsequently, are
the genuine arithmetic signatures: mirror-type signatures consonant with
the measured Frobenius spike $\operatorname{diag}(1,p^3,p^5)$, one floor
above the elliptic-modular sporadics; see the companion paper
\texttt{papers\_out/modular\_anchors} (conjectural tier) for the
precisely scoped statements. We record the induced expectation as the
\emph{mirror form of the bridge}: in the block's $q$-coordinate the
companion rows should be coefficient sequences of $q$-side Eichler-type
objects (as \eqref{eq:zetacomp} realises for the sporadic $\zeta$), and
\eqref{eq:bridge} the $t$-shadow of a $q$-side identity; together with
Theorem~\ref{thm:blind} this says the weight-five-seeing instrument
should be built from the block's own coordinates rather than binomial
letters.

\subsection{Outlook}
The natural home of the tower is the moduli-space family: parameter
derivatives are the Gauss--Manin connection, the recurrence is the
contiguity direction, and \eqref{eq:anchor} is their compatibility. The
jet-coaction dictionary (jets as de Rham shadows of the motivic coaction,
with the sporadic dichotomy as Galois equivariance) is conjectural but has
paid rent: it retrodicts the one-step (unipotent) shape of
\eqref{eq:anchor} --- the inhomogeneity couples to $Q$ alone --- and it
makes the falsifiable prediction that second variations anchor in
$\operatorname{span}\{Q,\dot Q\}$ only, which the tower's second rung will
test. The immediate mathematical targets are: a parametric certificate for
the cellular family; the second rung of the tower; and WZ certificates for
\eqref{eq:ann}. On the first target, Brown's recent Mellin-transform
framework \cite{Br26} provides, in its appendix for the
$\zeta(2)$/$\mathcal M_{0,5}$ case, exactly the required object in a toy:
explicit contiguity matrices $M_i(s)$, \emph{rational in continuous
parameters}, transporting an anchored period vector across the parameter
lattice; differentiating the rational transport chain anchors
parameter-derivatives in closed form. Constructing the analogous matrices
for the $\zeta(5)$/$\mathcal M_{0,8}$ family is the identified one-time
cost of the tower programme (see \texttt{work/BROWN26\_MINING.md}).
