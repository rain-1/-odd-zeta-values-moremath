# Hostile referee report — `papers_out/sporadics/main.tex`

**Draft refereed:** first complete draft, 20 pp., compiled clean.
**Mode:** adversarial. Attack surface: (i) overclaiming, (ii) label drift
(`[VERIFIED]` restated as proved), (iii) citation and novelty errors, (iv) gaps in the
written proofs. Every external claim was re-checked against a fetched source.

**Verdict on the draft as submitted: REJECT pending repair.** Two novelty claims are

## MAJOR

### R1. The Apéry-limit corollary is *not* new. `[novelty error]`

The draft's Corollary (Apéry limits, `cor:limits`) says
`lim B^(d)(n)/A^(d)(n) = zeta(2)/(d+1)` for `d = 3, 4` and remarks that the `d = 4` case
"appears not to have been proved before", on the strength of Chamberland–Straub's
"we expect that it can be established".

**This is false.** A. Straub and W. Zudilin, *Sums of powers of binomials, their Apéry
limits, and Franel's suspicions*, arXiv:2112.09576, IMRN **2023** no. 11, 9861–9879,
prove it for **every** `s`. Their Theorem 1.3 plus Example 1.5 read verbatim:

> `lim B^(s)(n)/A^(s)(n) = 1/(s(s+1)) * (s/6) pi^2 = zeta(2)/(s+1)`,
> "which matches [CS21, Conjecture 9]".

and they explicitly note "The case s = 3 was previously proved by Zagier [Zag09] using
modular forms." Fetched from the arXiv PDF; the passage is quoted in full in the repair
note below.

**Repair:** attribute the theorem to Straub–Zudilin, delete the novelty claim, and present
the corollary as an independent elementary re-proof from the closed form (which it is: the
Straub–Zudilin argument goes through a locally uniform limit of an analytic deformation,
ours through a concentration estimate on an explicit finite sum).

### R2. The Franel and `s10` closed forms are also in the literature. `[novelty error]`

Worse: the draft's symmetric weight is *literally* Straub–Zudilin's object. Their
deformation (their eq. (6)–(7))

```
A^(s)(n,t) = sum_k C(n,k)^s prod_{j<=k}(1-t/j)^{-s} prod_{j<=n-k}(1+t/j)^{-s}
           = sum_{j>=0} A_j^(s)(n) t^{2j}
```

expands as `exp(s t delta + s t^2 sigma/2 + O(t^3))` with `delta = H_k - H_{n-k}`,
`sigma = H2_k + H2_{n-k}`, so

```
A_1^(s)(n) = sum_k C(n,k)^s ( s sigma/2 + s^2 delta^2/2 ),      A_1^(s)(1) = s(s+1),
B^(s)(n) = A_1^(s)(n)/(s(s+1)) = sum_k C(n,k)^s ( alpha_s sigma + beta_s delta^2 )
```

with `alpha_s = 1/(2(s+1))`, `beta_s = s/(2(s+1))` — the draft's Theorems `thm:franel`
and `thm:s10` exactly. **Checked numerically:** exact rational agreement for
`s = 3,4,5,6`, `n <= 12` (`scratchpad`, truncated power series over `Fraction`).

**Repair:** state the identification, attribute, and keep as the genuine contribution
(a) the explicit finite certificate proof, which is valid for **every** `n >= 1` where
Straub–Zudilin's Theorem 1.3 gives the recurrence only "for large enough `n`", and is
Lean-ready; (b) the harmonic-monomial *display*, which is what Theorem LB consumes;
(c) Proposition `prop:boundary` (the boundary identities are forced for every `d`).
Downgrade the corresponding bullet in the introduction.

### R3. `[PROVED]` on the dichotomy observation is label drift. `[label]`

`obs:dichotomy` ("a pure harmonic monomial can never produce `L(chi,w)` in the limit")
carries `[PROVED]`. It is not proved and cannot be with present technology: it needs
`L(chi,w)` to lie outside the Q-algebra generated by the zeta values (and `log 2`), and
already the irrationality of Catalan's constant `G = L(chi_{-4},2)` is open. This is
precisely the failure mode the labelling discipline exists to prevent.

**Repair:** demote to a heuristic with the transcendence input stated as an assumption;
soften the two downstream appeals (`prop:C`, §`sec:E`) from "must" to "expected".

### R4. The integrality corollary does not follow from Theorem LB. `[label + gap]`

`cor:integrality` (`v_p(B(n)) >= -w floor(log_p n)`) is labelled `[PROVED]` and derived by
"iterating the ratio form of Theorem LB". Theorem LB is a **single-digit** statement: its
hypothesis (H4) is used in the form `x(n,k) <= n < p^2`, which is exactly `a < p`. The
iteration would require the statement at `a >= p`, where the descended letters
`K^(r)(floor(x/p))` are no longer `p`-integral, so the proof does not iterate. Even
granting the master form, the ratio form needs `A(n)` and `A(q)` to be `p`-units, which is
not asserted anywhere.

**Repair:** restate as an Observation labelled `[VERIFIED]` (which is what the source memo
actually says), with a remark on the heuristic derivation and the `p`-unit hypothesis.
Adjust §`sec:BV` item 2, which leans on it, to match.

## MODERATE

### R5. The Gosper degree bound is misapplied. `[gap]`

`thm:gosper` argues "`a(k) - b(k-1)` has degree 2 with leading coefficient `-n != 0`, so
the degree bound gives `deg x = 0`". That is the degree bound for the case
`deg(a - b^-) >= deg(a + b^-)`. Here `deg(a - b^-) = 2 < 3 = deg(a + b^-)`, so one is in
the *second* Gosper case: the coefficient of `k^{m+2}` in `a x(k+1) - b(k-1) x(k)` is
`x_m (m - n)`, and the bound `m = deg c - 2 = 0` follows only because `m = n` is
impossible. Over `Q(n)` with `n` an indeterminate that is automatic; **for an integer `n`
it is not**, and moreover `gcd(a(k), b(k+j)) = 1` fails at `j = n` for integer `n`. The
theorem is therefore a statement over `Q(n)` — i.e. it excludes any certificate rational
in `n`, which is what a uniform proof needs — and must say so.

**Repair:** rewrite the proof with the correct case of the degree bound and state the
scope (`Q(n)`) explicitly.

### R6. "if and only if" in `prop:boundary` is not proved. `[overclaim]`

The "only if" direction needs `H2_n, H_n^2, H_n, 1` to be linearly independent over
`Q(n)`. True (Karr/PiSigma theory) but not proved in the paper and not needed.

**Repair:** state the sufficient direction; note the necessity in a remark.

### R7. `thm:norepair` quantifies over more primes than were computed. `[scope]`

The statement says "the `F_p`-linear system is inconsistent" and the table covers
`p = 5,...,23` only; the sentence "for `E` the kernel has rank 0 against `Delta` at every
prime" is an extrapolation.

**Repair:** bound the statement to the tested primes; move the extrapolation into a
labelled remark.

### R8. `prop:s7` conflates verified with proved. `[label]`

"(V1) and (V2) ... give (LB) for `s7`" reads as a proof of (LB) for `s7`. (V1) and (V2)
are `[VERIFIED]` in a finite range, so what is available is (LB) in that range (which was
already verified directly) plus a *route*.

**Repair:** make the conditional explicit.

## WHAT SURVIVES THE ATTACK

For the record, the following were attacked and stand:

* The `(H2)` correction and the base-level defect `Delta(a)` (§4). Re-derived
  independently: for `gamma` at `p = 5`, `a = 3` the digits `b = 2, 3` are dropped and at
  `a = 4` the digits `b = 1,2,3,4` are, so the "product region `{0<=b<=a}`" of the earlier
  statement is false already for the Apéry `zeta(3)` pair. Lemma `lem:Deltatame` (the
  clause is free under tameness) is correct, so the four proved instances are unaffected.
* Theorem `thm:norepair` modulo R7 — a finite exact linear-algebra computation is a proof
  of the finite statement.
* Theorem `thm:gosper` modulo R5 — re-verified symbolically, including the stronger
  "no solution for any `deg x <= 8`".
* Proposition `prop:nolimit` (discriminants `-27, -128, -16`) — recomputed; and the
  remaining twelve discriminants are `81, 64, 125, 16, 1, 144, 1152, 512, 432, 784, 400,
  16`, all positive.
* Every certificate identity of §6.2–§6.3 — re-derived from scratch in a computer-algebra
  system and each returns 0; the boundary identities of `prop:boundary` were *derived*,
  not merely checked, and reproduce the published `N(n)`, `M(n)` for `d = 3` and `d = 4`
  exactly.
* The whole of §3 and §5's data: `4920` single-digit cells at `p = 5,7,11,13` across all
  fifteen families with `0` failures; master floor exactly `w` for all fifteen at
  `p = 5, 7`, all `n < p^3`; weights and their sharpness at `n <= 120`.
* The `E2` representation `A_E(n) = sum_k 4^{n-2k} C(n,2k) C(2k,k)^2` — recomputed,
  agrees for `n <= 30`.
