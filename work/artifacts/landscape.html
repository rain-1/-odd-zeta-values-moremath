The central discovery

The purity defect, in three costumes

Why does ζ(5) resist while ζ(3) fell? The rank-3 motive carries a middle graded piece (the ζ(3)-companion P̂) that the rank-2 Apéry motive simply doesn't have. We sighted its cost three times before interrogating each sighting — one identified, one confirmed, one retracted. The record keeps all three, because the retraction is part of the mathematics.

Analytic costume — confirmed

The middle-root exile

The operator contains a minimal ray decaying at 0.0050 < e⁻⁵ — fast enough to prove ζ(5) irrational. Every ℚ-rational (pure) class rides the middle ray instead. The ζ(2)-elimination that purifies a class is exactly the rotation off the fast ray.

Numeric costume — identified

c = −3/π² = 12/(2πi)²

Unmasked: a Tate twist, proved via BZ's own decomposition I = 2I′ + 4ζ(2)Î — the minimal ray is the unpurified cellular integral (441-digit certificate). PSLQ had missed it because a period basis can't see a reciprocal period. Purification costs exactly one ζ(2), in rate.

Algebraic costume — retracted

The depth-2 "obstruction" was an artifact

The claim that w₅ needs nested letters is false — a 130-term depth-1 weight function exists (found by CRT reconstruction, verified independently). The prior "proved-negative" capped monomials at three factors. The impurity is real in the periods but does not reach the letters.

One ratio, two completions

The mirror

The same extension class has two periods. On one side of the mirror everything is provable; on the other sits the open problem. The asymmetry has a name: positivity — an archimedean phenomenon with no p-adic counterpart.

Archimedean side · ℝ

  • Period: ζ(3), ζ(5) — irrationality open beyond Apéry
  • Positivity forces forms onto the middle ray (sector B, proved via I′ = I + ζ₂|I″|)
  • Purity tax charged in full: γ = 0.866 (w=5), γ₅,₇ ≤ 0.34 (w=7)
  • Rank 2 is the loophole — no middle ray exists, purity is free: that is Apéry's ζ(3)

p-adic side · ℚₚ

  • Tower limits Λₐ exist (exactly 3 digits/level) — but are not ζₚ(3): new intrinsic constants (order-3 operator can't reach ζₚ(3)'s Frobenius slot)
  • cₚ = 0: no p-adic purity defect — the diag(1, p³, p⁵) grading separates what degenerates archimedean-ly; and p-adic limits exist even where archimedean ones don't (B, δ, η)
  • No positivity ⟹ minimal rays reachable: ζ₂(5) is proved irrational (LSZ; their dₖ⁵ ledger confirmed tight, measures reproduced exactly)
  • But the linear forms diverge (n³): structurally clean, arithmetically weak — the exact chiasmus of the archimedean side. Deficits: ζ₅(3) by 0.586, ζ₃(5) by 0.606

Where this points

The programs, ranked by what they'd teach us

  1. Close Phase 2. Certify Theorem B, run the corrected middle-row ledger, find w₅. Yield: the first denominator theorem for M̄₀,₈ cellular periods, and the full rank-3 Frobenius congruence.
  2. The defect program. Identify c (Frobenius constants), vary it over the cone, then score Brown's entire cellular enumeration by defect — the first search surface aimed at the binding constraint. Jackpot odds are low; the tool is the point.
  3. The p-adic seam. Compute cₚ; if tame, p-adic minimal-ray forms exist and the ζ₂(5)-style program extends. Our congruence machinery is native here.
  4. The general (LBₔ) paper. Fifteen sporadic pairs, one theorem: the Frobenius ladder made elementary — and the coefficient form of Beukers–Vlasenko's open Conjecture 7.5 for the class.

Ruled out, with measurements rather than opinions: window theorems from the known families, orbit optimization at weight 7, constant-sharpening, and every direct attack on ζ(5) — the field's binding constraint is a new construction, and the defect is the first instrument that scores one.
