## T5/T2 — **the structure theorem** (what Λ actually is)

### Step 1. The harmonic split `[PROVED]`

Apéry's formula `b_n = Σ_k A(n,k)[H₃(n) + T(n,k)]`, `A(n,k) = C(n,k)²C(n+k,k)²`,
`T(n,k) = Σ_{m≤k}(−1)^{m−1}/(2m³C(n,m)C(n+m,m))`, splits `f = H₃ + c_n/a_n`. The p-divisible
layer of `H₃` gives `H₃(n) = p^{−3}H₃(⌊n/p⌋) + Σ_{k≤n, p∤k}k^{−3}`, hence **exactly**

> `p^{3s}H₃(a p^s) = Σ_{i=0}^{s} p^{3i} S_i(a)`, `S_i(a) = Σ_{k ≤ ap^i, p∤k} k^{−3}`,
> and, re-indexing `x = k/p^i`,
> ### `h_p(a) := lim_s p^{3s}H₃(ap^s) = Σ_{x ∈ ℤ[1/p], 0 < x ≤ a} x^{−3}`.

`[VERIFIED]` against `p^{3s}H₃(ap^s)` for `a = 1,2,3`, `s ≤ 3`, p = 5,7,11,13: agreement
4, 9, 14, 19 digits (the harmonic tower converges at **5** digits/level at weight 3 — faster than
the Apéry tower, see the rate law). Only `i < K/4` terms are needed for K digits, so `h_p` is
computable to 40+ digits in milliseconds.

### Step 2. The Kazandzidis limits and their closed form `[PROVED + VERIFIED]`

`c_p(A,B) := lim_t C(Ap^t, Bp^t)` exists by Jacobsthal–Kazandzidis (3 digits/level `[VERIFIED]`).
Morita's factorisation `n! = p^{v_p(n!)}∏_{i≥0}(−1)^{n_i+1}Γ_p(n_i+1)` (`n_i = ⌊n/p^i⌋`) plus
`Γ_p(1+x) = −Γ_p(x)` on `pℤ_p` and Beukers–Vlasenko eq. (2)
`log Γ_p(x) = Γ′_p(0)x − Σ_{m≥2}ζ_p(m)x^m/m` (the `Γ′_p(0)` terms cancel because `A = B+(A−B)`,
and `ζ_p(even) = 0`) give the boxed formula of the headline.

   Frobenius structure as `A(y_i(t^p)) = p^i Σ_j α_j y_{i−j}(t)`, so an order-3 MUM operator has

   the slots are literally zero. **`Λ` is definitely not an `α_j`.** (This confirms and sharpens

2. **But `Λ` and the `α_j` are two extractions from the *same* function.** BV take **Taylor

   which our constants are "p-adic Frobenius data" — and why they are not ℚ-affine in any single
   `ζ_p(m)`.
3. **Terminology to keep straight (a real collision):** Bloch–Vlasenko / Roy–Vlasenko
   *Frobenius **constants*** `κ_n` are archimedean periods (expansion coefficients of the
   generalised gamma function `κ(s) = s²∫₀¹t^{s−1}δ(t)dt`), conjecturally ℚ-combinations of
   products of zeta values of total weight n; Beukers–Vlasenko *Frobenius **structure**
   constants* `α_j` are the p-adic ones above. **Our `Λ_a` is the p-adic analogue of the third
