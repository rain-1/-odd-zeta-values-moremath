state and file pointers. House rules apply to any successor: exact arithmetic,

## Tier 1 — would unify or complete major structures

1. **The two DIG lemmas** (work/DIG_SCAN.md §top-open): rule R2 (a
   Nesterenko/Zudilin-style denominator lemma) and the kernel inclusion
   ker ρ_{w′} ⊆ ker ρ_w. Proved, they turn the p-adic rank wall into a
   theorem that is the exact p-adic analogue of why "one of ζ(5)…ζ(11)" has
   never been sharpened — one statement unifying both sides of the mirror.
   The single highest-value open item.

2. **The missing 3.** The sharp-12 factor's 3-part is attained but underived:
   July's H2 analysis PROVED the geometric (Betti-lattice) mechanism cannot
   produce a 3 (H2_SIGNS, quoted in Paper B §15). Where does it come from?
   (Candidate: the Bernoulli/period normalization, not the geometry — but
   that is a guess with no surface attached yet.)

3. **Theorem B certification** (PHASE2_CERTS §19; CERTS_RESUME §12): one
   route, time-bound not diverging (Annihilator[T·ṽ] > 90 min at ≤ 5 GB;
   a 5.5-hour run in flight at close-out). (T1-top) is structurally blocked
   (Θ(ρ,σ) coefficients for every non-constant weight; degree-≤3/refold/
   piece routes all excluded) — needs a genuinely new certificate idea.
   The p ≥ 5 theorem's mathematics is complete regardless.

4. **The conservation law as a theorem**: r = 1 + ⌊m/2⌋ (GAMMA_UNIFICATION
   §T4) — verified on four families with a successful ζ(7) prediction;
   stated with three falsification conditions. Prove it for the cellular
   class; connect to the rank-2-is-tax-free explanation of Apéry.
