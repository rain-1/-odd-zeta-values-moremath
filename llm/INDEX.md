## Conversion modes

- `pandoc-flat` — arXiv LaTeX flattened (`\input` inlined, comments stripped) and converted by pandoc. Math is preserved as LaTeX inside `$`/`$$`.
- `pandoc-repaired` — same, after neutralising constructs pandoc's reader rejects (custom `\section(...)` arguments, block environments nested in `\textit`).
- `latex-verbatim` — pandoc could not parse the source; the cleaned LaTeX is included verbatim in a fenced block. Still readable, just noisier.
- `pdftotext -layout` — no TeX source exists. Formulas are flattened to approximate plain text and are **lossy**; consult the PDF for anything load-bearing.
